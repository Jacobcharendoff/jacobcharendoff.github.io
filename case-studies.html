Challenge

A high-growth platform at $30M ARR with revenue leaking between stages. Pipeline, forecast, and handoffs were each breaking in their own way, and leadership couldn't trust the number.

Action

Brought in by the CEO to own Sales, Marketing, and Customer Success across a 30-person org. Rebuilt the GTM system end to end: standardized stages, installed deal-review discipline, fixed the Sales-to-Onboarding handoff, and put weekly revenue reconciliation in place.

Result

20% ARR growth to $36M, net revenue retention at 112%, and 35+ new logos, all in under a year. Forecast accuracy past 90% within two quarters. Handoff time cut 75% (48 to 12 hours) and late-stage pipeline completeness up from 55% to 91%.

Challenge

A standing start. No funding, no team, no playbook, founded on the side while I was still running revenue at Berman.

Action

Owned everything commercial: pricing, product bundling, lifecycle, and a repeatable acquisition model with tight unit economics. Challenge

Hired as the first commercial hire, pre-revenue, for a family-backed portfolio in health tech and hospitality. The job grew from proving the model to making two companies sellable.

Action

Built the first CRM and revenue reporting from scratch and grew the original business to its first seven figures. Promoted to Director of Operations to stabilize delivery as revenue tripled, then to VP, Revenue & Growth across both companies. Led the ground-up commercial build for StarDome Peru and restructured pricing across the portfolio to smooth seasonality and survive buyer scrutiny.

Result

Scaled StarDome to $22.5M USD in revenue at a ~22% EBITDA margin (~$5M EBITDA) and grew the team from 9 to 50+. Ran board reporting, due diligence, and transition planning for two exits totaling $28M+: an $8.5M health tech transaction and a ~$19.5M hospitality sale at roughly 4x EBITDA.
